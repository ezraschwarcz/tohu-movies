Cookie Notice

Tohu Movies · Last updated November 9, 2026 · Contact: privacy@tohumovies.com

This notice explains what cookies Tohu Movies uses on tohumovies.com, why we use them, and how you can manage them. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a website you visit. Cookies are widely used to make sites work, remember preferences, understand how a site is used, and serve advertising. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, web beacons, and device identifiers, which are covered by this notice where they serve a similar purpose.

Cookies we use

Essential

Strictly necessary cookies

Needed for the site and player to work and to stay secure, for example to load pages, play video, maintain a session, and prevent fraud and abuse. The site cannot function properly without these. They do not store personally identifiable information for marketing.

Functional

Functionality and preference cookies

Remember choices you make, such as playback or display preferences, so you do not have to set them again on each visit. This includes your star ratings for films and your age confirmation for R rated films, which are stored only in your browser’s local storage and are never sent to us. Disabling these may mean some preferences are not remembered.

Performance

Performance and analytics cookies

Help us count visits, understand which films and pages are watched, and improve the player and catalog. Information is generally aggregated, but it may include device, browser, and usage data.

Advertising — Google

Advertising cookies from Google and its partners

Tohu Movies uses Google Ad Manager to manage ads and Google AdSense as a source of ads. Google and its partners use advertising cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie where applicable, to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites, to personalize ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure ad performance, and to detect fraud. Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

Personalized advertising

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and / or other sites on the internet. Those ads may be personalized ads.

Opt out of personalized ads: Visit Google Ads Settings to turn off personalized advertising. You can also opt out of some third party vendors’ cookies at aboutads.info/choices. You will still see ads after opting out, they just may be less personalized.

Learn more in Google’s Advertising Technologies explanation and How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

How to manage cookies

In your browser

Most browsers let you view, block, and delete cookies. You can usually find these controls in Settings under Privacy or Cookies. Blocking all cookies may break playback or other features. Guides are available from your browser maker:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Settings / Preferences → Privacy
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

For advertising

  • Google Ads Settings: turn off ad personalization.
  • aboutads.info/choices: opt out of personalized ads from participating companies.
  • On mobile devices, check your device’s ad privacy settings to limit ad tracking or reset your advertising identifier.

If you clear cookies or use a different browser or device, you may need to set your choices again.
